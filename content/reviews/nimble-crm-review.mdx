## One plan is a real advantage — right up to the second list

Nimble does something the rest of this category does not: it sells **one plan**.

**$29.90 per seat per month billed monthly, or $24.90 billed annually** — about a 17% saving. That is the whole tier structure. No Starter/Professional/Enterprise ladder, no feature two tiers up, no working out which column you belong in.

For anyone who has spent an afternoon comparing three-column pricing grids, that simplicity is worth something real.

Then you scroll, and there is a second price list:

| Add-on | Price |
|---|---|
| Email Marketing | **$15 / company / month** |
| Web Forms & Web Chat | **$12 / team / month** |
| Data Enrichment | **$10 / month** (100-credit increments) |
| Additional 10,000 contacts | **$10 / month** |
| Additional 10 GB storage | **$10 / month** |

None of that is hidden — it is all published. But **the honest price of this product is the plan plus whichever of those five you actually need**, and the $29.90 figure that circulates is the first line only.

## The 1,000-message ceiling is the one to check first

Included group messaging is **1,000 messages per licence per month**.

That is the number most likely to surprise you, because "CRM with email" implies emailing your contacts, and 1,000 messages against 25,000 included contact records is a ratio of **one message per 25 contacts per month**.

The upgrades are published:

- **$15** for 2,000 emails
- **$69** for 10,000 emails
- **$99** for 25,000 emails

Note the shape of that curve. Per email, $15/2,000 is $0.0075; $99/25,000 is $0.0040. It gets cheaper with volume, which is fair — but emailing your full 25,000-record database once a month is **$99/month on top of every seat**, and that is a different product to the one the headline price describes.

**Work out your monthly send volume before anything else here.** It is the number that decides whether this is a $29.90 tool or a $130 one.

## Two seats and a real send list, priced honestly

A two-person team wanting the CRM, email marketing and 10,000 sends a month:

```
2 seats × $24.90 (annual)      $49.80
Email Marketing add-on         $15.00
Group messages, 10,000         $69.00
                              --------
                              $133.80 / month
```

**$1,605.60 a year** — a defensible price for what it does, and 2.7× the seat-only figure. That is the number to carry into a comparison, not $29.90.

## The 25,000 records are genuinely generous

Worth saying clearly, because the add-on list above reads critically and the base allowance deserves credit.

**25,000 contact records included** at under $30 a seat is a lot. Several competitors meter contacts far more aggressively, and the overage price here — **$10/month per additional 10,000** — is mild rather than punitive.

Storage is thinner: **2GB per user**, upgradeable at $10/month per 10GB. For a CRM storing notes and the occasional attachment that is fine; for one where people attach proposals and decks it is a line you will cross.

## What the single-plan model actually costs you

The trade for simplicity is that there is no cheaper tier to start on. Every seat is $29.90 or $24.90 from day one, whether that person is a full-time salesperson or someone who looks at the pipeline on Fridays.

Tiered competitors let you mix — a couple of full licences and several cheap view-only ones. Nimble does not offer that on this page, so **occasional users cost the same as daily ones**, and a team with several light-touch participants pays a premium for the simplicity.

A previous version of this review called the social data enrichment "uniquely valuable." **That was never sourced and has been removed** — enrichment quality varies by contact and region, and a claim of uniqueness would require testing competitors' enrichment side by side, which nobody here has done. What is on the record is the price: **$10/month per 100 credits.**

## Who should not buy this

**If you need marketing automation as the core of the job**, this is a relationship CRM with an email add-on, and the 1,000-message base allowance shows where the product's centre of gravity sits.

**If most of your team are occasional users**, a single-price-per-seat model charges full freight for people who log in twice a month.

## The honest summary

**One plan at $24.90 annually with 25,000 records is clean, simple pricing, and the add-on prices are all published rather than quoted.**

**Just build the whole bill before comparing.** Seats, plus email marketing at $15, plus whatever your real send volume costs — for a two-person team sending 10,000 emails a month that is $133.80, not $29.90.

*The single Business plan price ($29.90 monthly, $24.90 annually), 25,000 contact records, 2GB per user storage, the 1,000-messages-per-licence-per-month group messaging limit, and every add-on price — email marketing $15/company/month, web forms and web chat $12/team/month, data enrichment $10/month per 100 credits, $10/month per additional 10,000 contacts, $10/month per additional 10GB, and group message upgrades at $15, $69 and $99 — are from Nimble's own pricing page, checked 11 August 2026.*

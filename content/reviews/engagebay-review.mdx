## The inversion on this pricing page

Read these two rows together, because their relationship is backwards from every other tool in this category:

- **Free** — $0, **250 contacts**, *free for teams up to 15 members*
- **Basic** — **$14.99 per user/month**, **500 contacts**

The free plan is the one with generous seats. The moment you pay, every head starts multiplying — and what the first paid tier buys you is **250 extra contacts**.

For a five-person team, moving from Free to Basic is **$74.95/month, or $899.40 a year, to go from 250 contacts to 500.** That is the arithmetic nobody does before signing up, and it is the single most useful thing to know about this product.

## The full ladder, and where the seats bite

| Plan | Per user/month | Contacts | 5-person team |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | 250 | **$0** (up to 15 people) |
| Basic | $14.99 | 500 | **$74.95** |
| Growth | $64.99 | 5,000 | **$324.95** |
| Pro | $119.99 | 50,000 | **$599.95** |

Prices shown are the paid-yearly rates; a biennial commitment brings Basic to $12.74, Growth to $55.24 and Pro to $101.99 per user.

**Growth is where most growing businesses land**, because 500 contacts is a very low ceiling and 5,000 is a real list. At five seats that is **$3,899.40 a year** — a genuine budget line, and a long way from the $14.99 that appears in comparison articles.

## What "free for teams up to 15" actually unlocks

The free tier is not a demo. The page lists it as including email marketing, autoresponders, email broadcast, sequences, lead grabbers, landing pages, CRM, helpdesk and live chat — with 250 contacts and 1 landing page.

That combination is unusual. Helpdesk and live chat on a free plan, for up to fifteen people, is more than most vendors give away, and for a small team with a small list it is a complete operating setup at zero cost.

**The binding constraint is 250 contacts, and it binds fast.** Two hundred and fifty is a few months of a working signup form. The honest way to use this tier is as a real trial of the whole product with your actual team, and to know in advance that the contact counter is what will end it.

## Price it per person before comparing anything

The most common error when comparing this against an all-in-one competitor is comparing a per-user price against a per-account price.

EngageBay's paid tiers are **per user**. If a rival charges per account, then at one seat EngageBay looks cheaper and at eight seats it may not. Work out your seat count first and multiply, then compare — the same discipline that the tiers themselves reward.

A previous version of this review claimed EngageBay delivered all-in-one at "a fraction of HubSpot's price." **That comparison was never sourced and has been removed.** Comparing two per-seat products requires both current price lists and a fixed seat count, and asserting a ratio without them is guesswork dressed as analysis.

## The 1-landing-page detail

Free includes **1 landing page**. Not one at a time — one.

For anyone planning to test offers, that is the practical end of the free plan well before the 250-contact cap. Landing-page testing is inherently comparative: you need a version A and a version B, and one page cannot give you that.

If landing pages are why you are here, treat Free as a look at the builder rather than a place to run campaigns.

## Who should not buy this

**If you need one thing done well**, an all-in-one that includes CRM, marketing, helpdesk and live chat is a lot of surface area to administer for a single job. Breadth has an operating cost even when the licence is free.

**If your list is already past 5,000**, the honest comparison is Pro at $119.99 per user, and at that price the field is wide open — you are no longer shopping in the budget tier the free plan implies.

## The honest summary

**The free plan is one of the more genuinely useful ones in this category** — fifteen seats, and the full product surface rather than a crippled slice.

**But the paid ladder is per user, and the contact allowances at the bottom are small.** $14.99 buys 500 contacts per person paying; a five-person team on Growth is $324.95 a month. Count your seats, count your contacts, and multiply before comparing this to anything.

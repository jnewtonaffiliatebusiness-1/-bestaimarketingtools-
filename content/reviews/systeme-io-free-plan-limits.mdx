## What the free plan actually includes

From Systeme.io's own pricing page, not a comparison blog:

| | Free |
|---|---|
| **Price** | **$0, permanent** |
| Contacts | **2,000** |
| Sales funnels | **3** |
| Courses | **1** |
| Emails | **Unlimited** |
| Affiliate program | **1, included** |
| Transaction fees | **None** |
| Credit card required | **No** |

**The free plan never expires and does not require a credit card.** That is the company's own wording, and it is the part that separates this from a trial.

## Why this is unusual

**ClickFunnels has no free plan at all** — entry is $97/month. Most platforms that do offer one give you a 14-day trial, a contact cap under 500, or strip email sending.

Systeme.io gives you **unlimited emails on the free tier.** For a 2,000-person list, that is not a token — you can email daily and never hit a wall. The cost of a comparable send volume elsewhere is usually the entire reason people start paying.

**Affiliate management on a free plan is genuinely rare.** ClickFunnels does not include it on its $97 Launch tier. If you sell anything with a referral component, that alone can be the deciding factor.

## Where it actually stops

**Three funnels.** That is the ceiling, and almost nobody hits 2,000 contacts before they hit it.

A funnel gets consumed faster than you expect: an opt-in page and thank-you sequence is one. A sales page with an order form is two. A webinar registration is three. **You are done, and your list might still be at 300.**

That is the honest read. The free plan is not limited by audience size — it is limited by how many distinct offers you can run at once.

**One course.** Fine for validating a single product. If you sell two, you are on a paid tier.

## What paying actually buys

| | Free | **Startup $17** | Webinar $47 | Unlimited $97 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Contacts | 2,000 | **5,000** | 10,000 | Unlimited |
| Funnels | **3** | **Unlimited** | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Courses | 1 | 5 | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Emails | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |

**The jump from free to $17 is almost entirely about funnels**, and that is the constraint that will actually bite you. Contacts go 2,000 → 5,000, which matters later; funnels go 3 → unlimited, which matters immediately.

**$17 is the tier most people should be on**, and they usually arrive there because of funnel count rather than list size.

## The honest way to use it

**Do not treat the free plan as a trial to evaluate.** Treat it as somewhere to build.

Put a real offer on it. Send real emails to a real list. Find out whether the rigid page builder actually bothers you and whether shallow reporting actually costs you anything — because for a lot of small operations, neither does.

**If you are currently paying for a platform, build your main funnel here in parallel before you cancel anything.** Migration is where switching goes wrong. Running both for a month costs you nothing on this tier, and it turns a leap into a comparison.

You will know within a fortnight whether the ceiling is a problem. If it is, it is $17.

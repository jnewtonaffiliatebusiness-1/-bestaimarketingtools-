## The number, and where it comes from

**Systeme.io Startup: $17/month. ClickFunnels Launch: $97/month.** That is **$80 a month, $960 a year**, and it is the entire reason this comparison gets searched.

Both figures are taken from each company's own pricing page, not from a comparison blog. That matters, because most of the articles ranking for this question quote $27 for Systeme.io — a tier that does not exist — and arrive at $840. The real gap is larger.

## What the extra $960 actually buys you

This is the part most comparisons skip. ClickFunnels is not simply more expensive for the same thing.

| | Systeme.io Startup | ClickFunnels Launch |
|---|---|---|
| **Price** | **$17/mo** | **$97/mo** |
| Contacts | 5,000 | **10,000** |
| Funnels | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Emails | **Unlimited** | 50,000/month |
| Courses | **5** | 3 |
| Workspaces | — | 1 |
| Custom domains | — | 5 |
| Affiliate management | **Included** | Not included |
| Free plan | **Yes, permanent** | No |

**ClickFunnels wins on one thing at this tier: contacts.** Ten thousand against five. If your list is already past 5,000, that is a real advantage and you should weigh it honestly.

**Everything else at this tier favours Systeme.io** — and the email cap is the one people discover late. Fifty thousand sends a month sounds generous until you email a 10,000-person list five times.

## Who should pay the extra $960

**Stay with ClickFunnels if:**

- Your list is over 5,000 and growing fast — you will hit Systeme.io's contact ceiling and need the $47 tier anyway, narrowing the gap
- Page design is your product. ClickFunnels' builder is better, and pretending otherwise would be dishonest. If your funnels are visually distinctive and that is part of why they convert, a rigid builder is a real cost
- You depend on a specific integration or template ecosystem that Systeme.io does not have

**Move to Systeme.io if:**

- Your list is under 5,000 and you are paying $97 for headroom you are not using
- You email frequently — unlimited beats a 50,000/month cap the moment you send often
- You want affiliate management without a separate line item. On ClickFunnels Launch you simply cannot have it
- You sell courses. Five against three, at a fifth of the price

## The honest test

Open your current ClickFunnels bill and find your actual contact count and monthly send volume. Not your plan limits — your usage.

If you are under 5,000 contacts, the $960 a year is buying you a better page builder and nothing else. That may still be worth it to you. **It is a real trade, not a trick** — but you should make it deliberately rather than by default.

If you are over 10,000 contacts, this comparison is not really about Launch, and you should be comparing Systeme.io's $47 Webinar tier against ClickFunnels Scale at $197.

## Try it before you switch

Systeme.io's free plan is not a trial. **2,000 contacts, three funnels, one course, unlimited emails, one affiliate program, no card required, no expiry.**

That is enough to rebuild your main funnel and send to a segment of your list before you cancel anything. Migration is where switching goes wrong — do it in parallel, not as a leap.

## The send limit is defined as a multiple

Most email tools publish a send cap as a fixed number. Benchmark publishes a **formula**:

> Monthly send limit: **10x contact limit**

So the allowance moves with your list. At 2,500 contacts it is 25,000 sends; at 100,000 contacts it is a million.

**Ten times sounds generous until you divide it by a month.** Ten sends per contact per month is roughly **two emails a week per subscriber** — comfortable for a monthly newsletter, tight for anyone running a weekly send plus an automated welcome sequence plus a re-engagement flow.

Do that arithmetic against your actual calendar rather than against the multiplier, because the multiplier is designed to look large and the calendar is what consumes it.

## Free is real, and it has one user

| | Free | Pro |
|---|---|---|
| Price | **$0/mo** | **from $27/mo** |
| Contacts | Up to **500** | 2,500 – 100,000 |
| Monthly sends | **2,500** | **10× contacts** |
| Users | **1 user max** | 1 included |
| Extra users | **cannot invite** | **$15/mo each, max 10** |
| Domains | not stated | 10 max |

Note that the free plan's send allowance is **5× its contact limit**, not 10×. The ratio improves when you pay.

The sharper limit is the user one. Free is explicit that it is **1 user max** and that you **cannot invite additional users** — not a soft cap you bump into, a stated impossibility. For a solo operator that is fine. For anyone who wants a colleague to proofread before sending, the free plan cannot do it at any list size.

## Extra users are $15, and capped at ten

Pro includes one user. Each additional is **$15/month**, to a maximum of ten.

That is a real cost for a small marketing team:

| Team | Extra users | Added per month |
|---|---|---|
| 2 people | 1 | **$15** |
| 3 people | 2 | **$30** |
| 5 people | 4 | **$60** |

A five-person team on the entry Pro tier is **$27 + $60 = $87/month** — more than triple the headline. And the ten-user ceiling is a genuine limit rather than a pricing step: at eleven people, this product does not fit, and the page offers Enterprise and a sales conversation instead.

## Annual billing saves a stated $60

The page states switching to annual saves **$60 off the Pro plan each year**.

Against $27/month ($324/year), that is roughly **18.5%**, bringing the effective rate to about $22/month. It is a flat figure rather than a percentage, which means the saving is proportionally largest at the cheapest contact tier and shrinks in significance as your list — and therefore your bill — grows.

Small, clearly stated, and worth taking if you are staying a year.

## What is included everywhere, and what that implies

Benchmark states that the email editor, contact management, sign-up forms and reporting are included **in all tiers, free included**.

That is the right way round: the things you need in order to send at all are not gated. What the tiers sell is capacity — contacts, sends and seats — rather than access to features.

**It also means upgrading will not unlock a capability you are missing.** If the editor cannot do what you want at $0, it will not do it at $27 either. Test the builder on the free plan against a real campaign before paying, because the paid tiers buy you more of the same, not something different.

A previous version of this review said the tool "lacks modern features." **That was vague and unsourced, and has been removed** — a feature comparison requires naming the features and checking each against the vendor's current page. What can be stated from the pricing page is the capacity ladder above.

## Who should not buy this

**If you need more than ten people in the account**, the published plan caps out and you are into Enterprise and a sales call.

**If your sending pattern is bursty** — a big launch sequence, then quiet — the 10× monthly formula may not suit you, because unused allowance in a quiet month does not help you in a busy one. The page does not state whether unused sends roll over. **Ask before committing.**

## The honest summary

**The free tier is genuine and permanent at 500 contacts, and the all-tiers feature inclusion is honest design** — you are buying capacity, not unlocking gates.

**Read the multiplier and the seat prices.** 10× contacts is about two emails per subscriber per week; free cannot add a second user at all; and a five-person team on entry Pro is $87/month, not $27.

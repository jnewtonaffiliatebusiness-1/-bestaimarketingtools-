## Two prices, and the second one is easy to miss

Lemlist publishes a plan price and a credit price, and only the first appears in comparisons.

**The plans:**

| | Email | Multichannel |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly | **$69** | **$109** |
| Annual | **$55/mo** | **$87/mo** |
| Users | **Unlimited users** | **per user** |
| Volume | 50,000 emails/mo | Unlimited emails & messages |
| Senders | not stated | 5 senders per user |

**The credits**, which are what it costs to find anyone to send to:

| Action | Cost |
|---|---|
| Verified email | 5 credits |
| Phone number | 20 credits |
| Company visited your site | 20 credits |
| Hiring / job change signal | from 100 credits |
| Company fundraised | 100 credits |
| LinkedIn engagement signal | from 400 credits |

And the conversion Lemlist publishes: **1,000 credits = $10 = 200 emails or 50 phones.**

## Work out the real monthly number

Say you want 1,000 verified email addresses a month — a modest target for outbound.

**1,000 emails × 5 credits = 5,000 credits = $50.** On the Email plan at $55 annual, your actual monthly cost is **$105**, not $55. The credit spend is roughly the plan again.

Want phone numbers too? 200 phones is **4,000 credits = $40**. Now you are at $145.

None of this is hidden — the rates are printed. But **the plan price is what gets quoted, and it describes less than half of what you will spend** if you use the product the way it is designed to be used.

## The unlimited-users detail is genuinely unusual

Worth pausing on, because it cuts the other way.

**The Email plan states unlimited users.** In a category that almost universally prices per seat, that is a real structural advantage — a five-person outbound team on the Email plan pays $55/month total, where most competitors would charge five times something.

**Multichannel is priced per user.** So the moment you need LinkedIn and calling alongside email, the pricing model flips from flat to per-head, and a five-person team is 5 × $87 = **$435/month** before a single credit is spent.

That is the sharpest decision on this pricing page, and it is not the one the tier names suggest. **The question is not "do I need multichannel" — it is "do I need multichannel badly enough to change my pricing model from flat to per-seat."**

## Intent signals are the expensive currency

The credit table has a wide range in it, and the top end deserves attention.

A **LinkedIn engagement signal costs from 400 credits — about $4 per signal** at the published conversion. **Hiring and job-change signals start at 100 credits, roughly $1.** A company that visited your website is 20 credits, about 20 cents.

Intent data is the most genuinely valuable thing in modern outbound: knowing someone just raised money, changed roles or looked at your pricing page is worth far more than another verified address. The prices broadly reflect that, and they are honest about it.

But **$4 per LinkedIn signal is a research budget, not a rounding error.** A thousand of those is $4,000. Build the number into the plan before switching the feature on, because it is the one line here that can scale past the subscription without anyone noticing.

## What the page does not say

Lead-credit inclusions per plan are **not stated** — the pricing page publishes what actions cost but not how many credits, if any, each tier includes.

That is the gap between "this costs $55" and "this costs $105", and you cannot close it from the outside. **Ask what the plan includes before subscribing.** It is also worth asking whether unused credits roll over, which the page likewise does not state.

## Who should not buy this

**If cold outreach is not legal or appropriate for your market and list**, the tooling question does not arise yet. Consent rules vary sharply by jurisdiction, and a platform that finds and contacts people efficiently makes a compliance mistake efficiently too.

**If you have not written a message that works**, more addresses will not help. Outbound is a copy problem wearing a data costume, and buying 5,000 verified emails to send an ineffective sequence to is a fast way to burn a domain and a budget at the same time.

## The honest summary

**The Email plan at $55 annual with unlimited users is a genuinely strong structure** — flat pricing in a per-seat category, with published credit rates rather than a quote.

**But budget two lines, not one.** A thousand verified emails a month roughly doubles the bill, Multichannel switches you to per-user pricing, and a single LinkedIn intent signal is about $4. Get the included-credit number in writing first — it is the one figure that decides whether $55 means $55 or $105.

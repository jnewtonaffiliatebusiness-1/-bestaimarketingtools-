## A different pricing axis entirely

Almost every tool in cold email prices seats. Woodpecker prices **outcomes of the work**:

> Contacted prospects: **$7.00 per 100**

No plan names. No tier ladder. The meter is how many people you actually emailed.

And then two lines that are unusual enough to quote directly:

- **Unlimited team members** — free
- **Unlimited email accounts** — free

Read that against the rest of the category, where a competitor charges per user and gives **one mail account per user**, so a one-person operation running four sending addresses pays for four seats. Here, that same setup pays for zero extra seats.

**If your constraint is mailboxes and people rather than volume, this pricing model is structurally cheaper** — and that is a real architectural difference, not a discount.

## What the base includes

Stated as included before any add-on:

- **16,000** emails monthly
- **4,000** stored prospects
- **4** warm-ups
- **100** Lead Finder credits
- Catch-all email address verification — free
- Unlimited team members — free
- Unlimited email accounts — free

Note the distinction between **stored prospects (4,000)** and **contacted prospects ($7 per 100)**. You can hold a list without paying to have contacted it. That is the right way round — a database you have not emailed has produced nothing.

16,000 emails against 4,000 stored prospects is four sends per prospect, which lines up with a typical four-touch sequence. The allowances are coherent with each other, which is more than several competitors manage.

## The add-on list is long — price it before committing

Ten published add-ons, and a serious sender needs several:

| Add-on | Price |
|---|---|
| Email addresses (Google/Microsoft) | **$6/mo each** |
| Email addresses (Maildoso/Mailforge) | **$4/mo each** |
| Warm-ups beyond the 4 included | **$5/mo per account** |
| LinkedIn Outreach | **$29/mo per account** |
| Domains (new) | ~**$1/mo**, billed annually |
| Integrations / API / Webhooks | **$20/mo** |
| Dedicated servers | **$59/mo per server** |
| Agency Panel | **$27/mo per active client** |
| Lead Finder credits | from **$10 / 500 credits** |
| Outbound workshops | **$2,000 / 4 sessions** |

**"Unlimited email accounts free" and "$6/month per email address" are not in conflict** — connecting an address you already own is free; having Woodpecker provision one costs money. Worth reading carefully, because the two lines look contradictory at a glance and are not.

A realistic eight-mailbox setup: 8 provisioned addresses at $6 = $48, plus warm-ups on the four beyond the included ones at $5 = $20, plus integrations at $20. **That is $88/month before a single prospect is contacted.**

## Model your real month

Contacting 2,000 prospects in a month:

```
2,000 prospects ÷ 100 × $7.00       $140
provisioned mailboxes (8 × $6)       $48
extra warm-ups (4 × $5)              $20
integrations / API                   $20
                                   -------
                                    $228 / month
```

Against a per-seat competitor where eight sending addresses means eight seats, that comparison often lands in Woodpecker's favour — but **only if you do it with your own numbers**, because the variable here is prospects contacted and there it is mailboxes.

⚠️ The pricing page also displays an annual figure of "$0.01 per 100" alongside a 99% savings claim. **That is not credible as rendered and is almost certainly a display fault** — a 99% discount on the core meter would make the product effectively free. **Do not plan against it; check the annual toggle at checkout.** Every figure used above is the monthly rate.

## The agency line

**Agency Panel: $27/month per active client.**

For an agency running outbound for ten clients that is **$270/month** on top of prospect volume and mailboxes. It is priced per *active* client, which is fair — dormant accounts should not bill — but it makes client count a direct cost driver.

## Who should not buy this

**If your volume is unpredictable and occasionally huge**, a per-prospect meter turns a big month into a big invoice. Per-seat pricing caps your downside; usage pricing does not.

**If you send to a small warm list**, none of this machinery applies. Sequencing infrastructure exists to solve volume you do not have.

## The honest summary

**Free unlimited team members and email accounts is a genuine structural advantage in a category that meters both**, and paying per prospect contacted means a quiet month costs less rather than the same.

**The add-on list is where the real bill assembles.** Provisioned mailboxes at $4–$6, warm-ups at $5, integrations at $20, agency clients at $27 each. Build the whole figure with your own prospect volume before comparing — and ignore the annual "$0.01 per 100" until you have seen it confirmed at checkout.
